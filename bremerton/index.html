Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Bremerton

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
